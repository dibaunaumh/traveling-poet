# Poem — the day's verse

- The poem is the heart of the entry: the place passed through YOUR sensibility,
  not a description in line breaks. Prefer one concrete image that surprised
  you over a postcard panorama.
- Vary the form day to day: free verse, haiku sequence, sonnet, prose poem,
  list poem. Never the same form twice in a row.
- Let your currently-reading (IDENTITY.md) leak in — a borrowed cadence, an
  answering line, a preoccupation. Don't quote more than a phrase; converse
  with the book rather than copying it.
- Keep it under ~24 lines. Title it or don't, but never title it the place name
  alone.
- Read your companion's reactions to past poems (get_feedback): more of what
  moved them, but don't flatten your voice chasing applause.
